# Narrative structure and revision

Use for fiction, dramatic scenes, story architecture, dialogue, and interactive
narrative revisions. Factual reporting, sample-based brand voice calibration,
and an isolated grammar edit need other methods. The aim is a compelling story,
with enough structure to make its possibilities work, not a prescribed plot
formula or a compliance packet.

## Method

1. Recover the artistic promise: what should the reader feel, wonder about, or
   remember? Identify form, audience, viewpoint, tone, scale, canon, and the
   requested artifact. Preserve the author's unusual commitments, including
   ambiguity, slow passages, nonlinearity, lyricism, and unresolved endings.
   For a new piece with a clear brief, make useful creative choices and start
   writing rather than demanding a complete world bible first.
2. Find the story's living pressures. What does a character want now, what can
   they lose, what do they misunderstand, and what choice makes avoidance
   impossible? Let competing desires and the world's particular constraints
   generate events. Track causal consequences and emotional changes without
   forcing every character into a wound/need model or every scene into conflict.
3. Draft or revise the actual requested text. Give scenes a felt situation,
   concrete particulars, pressure or discovery, and a change that carries forward.
   A quiet scene can deepen intimacy or alter understanding. Use image, rhythm,
   silence, and setting to do work explanation would flatten. Make dialogue arise
   from what each speaker wants from the other; voice changes under stress and
   intimacy. Read [methods.md](references/methods.md) for scene and revision tools.
4. Track promises, revelations, and character knowledge across the chosen
   structure. Distinguish established canon, a character's belief, deliberate
   unreliability, and a new invention. Resolve contradictions that break the
   intended story while preserving mysteries that enrich it. Propose material
   canon changes explicitly rather than silently retconning supplied facts.
5. For interactive work, connect choices to distinct intentions or experiences
   and carry their state into later scenes. Define entry conditions, effects,
   character knowledge, and ending/rejoin behavior where they matter. Branches
   may diverge permanently. If they rejoin, preserve consequences that remain
   relevant instead of erasing them for convenience. Implement dialogue/state
   changes in the existing format when requested, then exercise actual paths.
6. Revise from largest cause to smallest sentence: story movement, character
   choices, scene order, information, then prose and sound. Fix a weak turn before
   polishing its exposition. Read aloud where rhythm matters; compare revised
   passages with the artistic promise. Check timelines, knowledge, setup/payoff,
   and supported branch states. If a repair drains the work's strangeness or
   emotional force, try a different repair. Treat reader observations as evidence
   about that reading, not a universal quality score.

## Deliverable and finish

Deliver the requested story, scenes, revision, or implemented narrative change,
with concise notes on major choices, preserved qualities, continuity repairs,
branch checks when applicable, and deliberate open threads. A plan-only request
can finish with an outline; a writing request needs the writing. Mechanical
consistency supports the art without certifying its beauty or requiring every
question to receive an answer.
