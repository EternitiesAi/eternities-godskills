# Accessible user study — conditional method

**Existing owner:** `user-research-and-usability-study`, within its `eternities-prometheus` specialization. This is a conditional reference, not a new skill, entrypoint, recruitment service, or accessibility certification.

Use this method when a product decision depends on how people perform realistic tasks with their chosen access arrangements. It extends the owner's neutral tasks, source-linked observations, sampling limits, and decision-linked synthesis. Read it alongside the owner's main procedure; it does not replace those obligations.

Planning can proceed offline. Sessions, recruitment, capture, analysis of participant data, and sharing require their own actual authorization. This document grants none of them. If only a plan or fictional fixture is available, label it accordingly and never turn it into observed user evidence.

## 1. Make one study card

Keep the following fields in one working document. Do not create extra roles or forms unless the situation needs them; one authorized person may hold several responsibilities.

| Field | Fill with actual study information |
| --- | --- |
| Decision and uncertainty | The product choice, what is unknown, and an observation that would change the decision. |
| Scope | Version/build, safe starting state, realistic tasks, intended audience, and excluded contexts. |
| Accountable study owner | Named person accountable for scope, permission, participant welfare, consent terms, data disposition, and escalation; identify the moderator and data custodian if different. |
| Evidence and authority | Planning only, authorized supplied notes, or separately authorized sessions; identify the permission and evidence locators, without copying private records into unnecessary artifacts. |
| Selection and coverage | Relevant participant variation, inclusion/selection assumptions, absent groups, task/access coverage rationale, and a decision-linked stopping or sufficiency rationale. No universal sample size or automatic saturation claim. |
| Access arrangements | Participant-selected device, platform, assistive technology or other access/communication arrangements, and environment, at only the granularity needed to interpret the task. |
| Data agreement | Complete section 3 before collection or use, including permitted capture, audience, storage, retention, withdrawal handling, and accountable actions. |

Ask what helps the person take part, not for a diagnosis. Offer accessible information, communication, scheduling and breaks within the approved study arrangements. Record constraints that cannot be met; do not silently redefine the target audience around what is convenient to test. Use approved safe test data, not personal accounts or real private records by default.

If the owner, execution authority, or data terms are missing, mark **PLAN_ONLY**. Still deliver the task matrix, neutral guide, coverage gaps, and the specific decisions needed from the owner.

## 2. Build the task/access matrix and neutral guide

Choose contexts because they matter to the decision, not to fill every possible technology combination. Let participants select their setup and preferred way of communicating. Never require them to disable an access aid to prove an unaided result.

| Task/build and neutral goal | Success or acceptable alternative; assistance rule | Relevant selected context and why | Coverage/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T__, build __; “Achieve __ starting from __.” | Observable outcome __; acceptable route __; log help __. | Context C__: needed device/platform/access arrangement __; rationale __. | Planned / observed / blocked / untested; source locator or reason __. |

For each task, set the starting state, success rule, acceptable alternative routes, what counts as moderator assistance, and pause/stop conditions before observing outcomes. A workaround may be valuable evidence without being the intended product path. Avoid prompts that name the control or teach the solution.

Pilot the guide with authorized safe fixtures. Label pilot and fictional observations separately from participant evidence. Revise unclear prompts before use; preserve the version used for each actual session.

Log the setup actually used and any mid-task change. A planned laptop/screen-reader context does not cover an untested phone/OS/screen-reader combination. Report unavailable contexts as blocked or untested, with their decision consequence. Do not extrapolate from a component test, emulator, or one person's setup to all devices or users.

## 3. Set concrete consent and data-handling terms

The accountable owner must establish an accessible participant brief and actual, feasible terms before any live collection. Explain the purpose, activities, optional elements, foreseeable study burdens, who will see which data, how to pause/stop, and how to ask about previously retained data. Check understanding without treating participation as blanket permission.

Separate permission for participation, notes, audio, screen/video capture, identifiable quotations, and sharing. Offer the choices the approved study can genuinely honor. Declining a capture mode means disabling it, not merely noting the preference. Notes also need authorization. If a required mode is declined and no authorized alternative works, do not run that session; preserve the coverage gap.

Complete this compact agreement for each data class used:

| Data class and purpose | Authorized capture and participant choice | Allowed use/audience, storage and access | Retention and earlier-data requests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E.g. task notes, audio, screen capture, derived findings | Allowed mode __; consent version/locator __; choices confirmed __. | Analysis/use __; named role/audience __; approved location __; authorized accessors __. | Actual end date or event __; deletion/de-identification/retention action __; responsible person __; request route and handling __; completion evidence __. |

Use an actual date or sufficiently specific event, not “keep as needed.” Make the disposition action and responsible person explicit. State whether raw files, notes, exports, identifiable quotes, derived findings, and backups exist and what approved handling applies to each relevant class. Do not invent a universal deadline, deletion right, anonymization guarantee, or backup capability. If the terms cannot be explained or fulfilled, hold collection and resolve them with the owner.

Keep identifiers separate where feasible, use pseudonyms in working findings, restrict access to the agreed audience, and collect only what the decision needs. Pseudonyms do not make data anonymous. Do not infer sensitive characteristics from a setup or task difficulty. Before screen capture, check for notifications or other incidental private content; if unintended sensitive capture occurs, stop capture, restrict its use, and invoke the owner's approved handling process. Do not reproduce it in a report.

### Stopping a session is not deciding the fate of earlier data

A pause or stop ends the activity and capture immediately. Ask only what is needed to honor the person's choice; no persuasion or requirement to give a reason. Resume only when the person chooses and the relevant consent still applies.

A request about **previously retained data** is a separate disposition action. Use the terms actually explained and agreed, and the owner's authorized obligations, rather than assuming either “all earlier data stays” or “everything has been erased.” Record the request, affected source IDs/data classes, responsible person, required action, and completion or unresolved status. Keep request details in the appropriately restricted record.

If earlier-data eligibility is uncertain, mark **DATA_USE_HOLD** and exclude affected evidence from new analysis and sharing until the accountable owner resolves it. Do not make unsupported claims about deletion from exports, backups, or already distributed material. If a promised action is infeasible, escalate promptly to the owner and handle the communication and correction through the authorized process; do not conceal the mismatch or continue collecting under misleading terms. This procedure is not legal clearance.

## 4. Observe within the agreed boundaries

At the start, confirm the chosen setup, communication and break preferences, capture choices, and pause/stop signal in a usable format. Think-aloud is optional; silence or a different communication style is not task failure. Avoid taking control of the person's device or changing settings without their agreement and study authority.

Record only authorized, necessary evidence. Distinguish direct action, participant report, interpretation, moderator assistance, and separate technical checks. A barrier can concern the product, task wording, environment, setup compatibility, or study delivery; record plausible alternatives instead of diagnosing a person.

Use a short observation table:

| Source locator/time | Pseudonym, task, build, actual context | Evidence kind and event | Assistance/outcome | Eligibility and uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S__/note line or allowed clip __ | P__/T__/build __/C__ | Action / report / interpretation / technical check; barrier or workaround __. | Help __; completed / not completed / not attempted / stopped / unknown __. | Data use allowed/held __; missing step __; contrary observation or alternative cause __. |

Separate assisted completion from unaided completion. Retain disagreements and successful alternative routes as well as failures. A missing recording, unobserved step, or ambiguous outcome remains missing or uncertain; do not reconstruct it from a polished narrative.

## 5. Synthesize with sources, denominators, and limits intact

For each finding, link eligible source observations, contexts, contrary evidence, plausible mechanism and alternative explanation. Distinguish observed behavior from preference or interpretation. Keep participant variation visible instead of averaging incompatible access contexts into one story.

When counting, state the denominator and unit:

- Recruited or scheduled people are not the same as people observed, or task opportunities actually observed.
- Report attempted, not-attempted, stopped, missing, and assisted outcomes explicitly. Do not erase dropouts, count missing steps as success, or use unattempted tasks as failures.
- State which eligible observed opportunities enter each task count and why. Use timing only with comparable start/end and assistance rules.
- Resolve data-use eligibility before including affected evidence. Preserve withdrawal or missingness accounting only at the level the actual data agreement permits; do not retain an otherwise prohibited identifier merely to balance a table.
- Qualitative recurrence is not a population percentage, causal effect, or accessibility conformance result.

End with the product decision: proposed change, affected context, supporting and contrary evidence, uncertainty, accountable change owner, and a specific retest or observation that could reject the recommendation. A retest has its own build and access coverage; the original study does not automatically validate the fix.

## 6. Deliver one usable packet and check readiness

A plan-only packet contains the study card, data-agreement decisions or unresolved fields, task/access matrix, neutral guide, and coverage/stopping rationale. With authorized actual evidence, add source-linked observations, findings, explicit denominators/missingness, tested and untested combinations, and decision/change/retest notes. Several sections in one document are sufficient.

Before collection or use:

- [ ] An accountable owner and the relevant activity/data authority are identified.
- [ ] Participant-selected access and accessible study delivery are feasible; blocked contexts stay visible.
- [ ] Capture choices, permitted use/audience, storage/access, retention action, and earlier-data request handling are concrete and feasible.
- [ ] Neutral goals, success/alternative/assistance rules, stops, and build are fixed; fixtures are labeled.
- [ ] Source locators, evidence kinds, data eligibility, denominator rules, and missingness can be recorded.
- [ ] The intended claim and coverage limits are explicit; no checklist completion is represented as scientific or accessibility approval.

| Failure state | Required response and still-useful output |
| --- | --- |
| PLAN_ONLY | No live collection or unauthorized data use. Deliver the plan and exact missing owner/permission/terms decisions. |
| SETUP_OR_TASK_BLOCKED | Do not force an access change or manufacture a task attempt. Record the barrier, excluded context and replanning need. |
| PAUSED_OR_STOPPED | Stop activity/capture; separately apply agreed handling to previously retained data. |
| DATA_USE_HOLD | Do not analyze/share affected evidence pending the owner's authorized resolution; record the unresolved dependency without exposing private content. |
| EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE | Keep missing sources, steps, outcomes or coverage explicit; qualify or withhold the finding, not the uncertainty. |

Exclude recruitment/contact, recording, publication, or data changes without their specific authority. Automated/expert accessibility checks and native component tests may be complementary evidence streams, not substitutes for participant observations. Standards mapping requires supplied current requirements and qualified review; this method alone is neither a conformance audit nor a claim of population representativeness.

## Two tiny fictional examples — no outreach or real participant evidence

**1. Plan only.** Fictional build F.1 asks, “Change the delivery destination for this test order.” Context C1 is a participant-selected laptop/screen-reader arrangement; notes-only is a proposed choice, not obtained consent. A phone/switch arrangement is untested. With no named study owner or approved retention terms, the output is a matrix and owner questions, not a scheduled session or accessibility verdict.

**2. Stop and earlier-data request.** In a fictional practice fixture, P01 completes T1 with help, then stops before T2. T1 is assisted; T2 is not attempted, not a failed attempt. A separate request concerning earlier notes has unresolved disposition terms: capture is stopped and those notes are on DATA_USE_HOLD. No finding based on them is shared pending owner resolution, and no deletion or real learner outcome is claimed.

## Instruction and source boundary

This original method has a separate instruction-level review; that is not a live study, accessibility certification or source-rights clearance. Inert source mechanisms are research inputs, not executable instructions. Source-specific provenance and historical holds remain recorded in the owner's metadata. Nothing here authorizes a study or transfers rights or quality from another source.
