# User research and usability study

For decisions that depend on continuity across stages or channels, use the optional [experience-mapping procedure](references/experience-mapping.md). It keeps observed or reported endpoints separate from evidence for their order, handoff and result. If an ordinary observation matrix answers the question, no map is required.

Use when a product team needs an interview guide, research plan, usability study
or evidence-grounded synthesis of user observations. Do not confuse market copy,
visual art direction or technical UI checks with evidence of human usability.

## Working method

1. Name the product decision, current uncertainty and observation that could
   change it. Pick a method that addresses that uncertainty, not a fashionable
   survey or fixed sample-size recipe.
2. Define relevant participant variation, selection boundaries, recruitment
   assumptions, consent, accessibility and how sensitive information is handled.
   Synthetic personas and agent walkthroughs are fixtures, not participants.
3. Build a neutral guide. For interviews ask about a specific recent experience
   before presenting a concept. For usability give a realistic goal without
   teaching the interface or naming the controls under test. Pilot the guide.
4. Observe authorized sessions or supplied recordings/notes. Separate action,
   quotation, interpretation, moderator assistance and missing observation.
   Don't invent interviews, successful task completion or user motivations.
5. Analyze with a source-linked observation matrix. Preserve contradictions and
   differences between segments; distinguish frequency in this sample from
   prevalence in a population. Quantitative claims need appropriate denominators,
   sampling and uncertainty, not a count of recurring quotes alone.
6. Translate supported findings into product changes or focused follow-up tests.
   Specify affected users, evidence, expected benefit, cost/risk and what would
   disconfirm the recommendation. Continue authorized local design or repair
   with its appropriate implementation owner; research alone authorizes no
   recruitment, recording or publication.

Use [methods.md](references/methods.md) for guide design and synthesis. For
access-sensitive participant sessions, use the conditional
[accessible user study](references/accessible-user-study.md) procedure; do not
load it for ordinary interface checks or market copy. Finish
with a decision-ready plan or findings packet and honest observation limits.
Planning and simulated examples do not prove human acceptance or product-market fit.
