# Serving-Time Ranking Decision Trace

**Status: DRAFT — independent first-party method candidate.** This is an explanatory review method for one named serving-time decision, not a ranking algorithm, provider integration, output schema, or evidence that recommendations are correct, fair, lawful, relevant, beneficial, or exposed to a person. It requires no provider or network. Source rights are not assessed or cleared by this method.

## Use only for this decision boundary

Use this route when a reviewer needs to explain one named per-request serving-time decision that produced a feed, recommendation list, or notification shortlist. Bind the trace to that request and decision time; do not generalize one trace to other requests, users, or releases.

This method does not own search reranking (retrieval-grounded answering), business or prospect scoring (Agora), external app-store placement (app-store discovery), offline scientific model validation (scientific-surrogate validation), or schema-only validation of a serialized record (structured-output-contracts). A task may need a separate owner for one of those questions; a valid record alone does not explain the decision.

## Trace one decision

1. **Name the boundary.** Record a stable, privacy-safe request/decision identifier, surface, decision timestamp and clock basis, serving boundary, and relevant release or configuration revisions. If the decision cannot be bound to one request, hold and ask for the missing boundary.
2. **Bind the candidate set.** Identify the source population and its exact revision or time-bounded snapshot, then account for the candidates entering this decision. Preserve stable item identifiers only when authorized; otherwise use an approved aggregate or pseudonymous form. A count or digest can help compare records but does not prove that the underlying source snapshot is immutable or complete.
3. **Show hard eligibility before scores.** State each applicable eligibility rule and its revision, and account for candidates as eligible, excluded with a reason, or unresolved. Apply hard constraints before scoring. An unknown or conflicting eligibility fact remains unknown and cannot be promoted by a high score; hold the affected decision when eligibility cannot be established.
4. **Bind decision-time inputs and scoring.** For every relied-on feature, record its definition/revision, value provenance or approved reference, and the latest time it was available to this decision. Identify missing, stale, future-dated, or contradictory values as such; do not silently replace them with zero or another default. Name the scorer or rule-set revision and the score inputs needed to explain the recorded result. Do not infer a model's validity from this trace.
5. **Reconcile post-score constraints.** Record the ordered scored candidates and each later cap, deduplication, diversity, tie-break, policy, or randomization rule with its revision. Account for final inclusion, exclusion, and order. A high score cannot override hard eligibility, and an unexplained change between scored and final order is a discrepancy, not a success.
6. **Separate requested from observed impressions and other effects.** Distinguish items requested for delivery, returned by the serving boundary, submitted for display/impression logging, acknowledged by a sink, and actually observed by an authorized measurement source. Bind each available event to the same decision/item and its timestamp. A request, response, attempted write, or acknowledgement is not by itself proof of a rendered or viewed impression; report missing or uncertain observation without converting it into exposure.
7. **Check privacy and consent.** Establish the allowed purpose, consent or other applicable collection basis, access boundary, retention, and permitted fields before reading or recording person-level evidence. Minimize the trace; prefer approved pseudonymous identifiers or aggregates. If authority, consent, or access is missing or contradictory, do not acquire or persist the personal data—hold and state the gap.
8. **Replay or reconcile without side effects.** Compare the trace against the exact candidate, feature, scorer, constraint, and configuration revisions recorded for that request. Replay only when the relevant inputs and execution semantics are available and replay is authorized; otherwise reconcile from preserved records and label the missing evidence. Do not silently substitute current inputs for historical ones, infer immutability from equal digests, or repeat an external effect to resolve uncertainty. Keep observed conflicts and unknowns visible.

## Return and limits

Return the bound request identity, candidate population and accounting, eligibility results, decision-time feature/scorer/constraint revisions, final ordered result, requested-versus-observed effect evidence, privacy/consent basis, replay or reconciliation performed, discrepancies, unknowns, and any hold. A missing field is a limitation, not permission to invent a value.

Completeness or internal consistency of this trace establishes only what the cited records support. It does not establish ranking correctness, fairness, legal compliance, relevance, user attention, live exposure, causal effect, source rights, or approval to read, write, publish, or contact a provider. No provider or network is required to apply the method; any separately requested live access or effect needs its own authority and owner.
