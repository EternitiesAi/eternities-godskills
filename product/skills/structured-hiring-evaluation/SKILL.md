# Structured hiring evaluation

Use when a team needs a hiring plan, interview scorecard, candidate debrief or
pipeline diagnosis. This is not a method for scraping candidates, inferring
protected traits, background checks, or making employment-law determinations.
A simple edit to a job title does not need this workflow.

## Method

Choose the requested operation: role/assessment design, candidate evaluation,
panel debrief, or recruiting-funnel diagnosis. Reuse established criteria when
reviewing a candidate; a funnel diagnosis need not redesign the interview process.
Apply the relevant steps below rather than generating every artifact.

1. Define the work the role must accomplish, constraints, decision owner and
   job-related criteria. Separate essential requirements from teachable skills.
   Replace vague “culture fit” with observable collaboration requirements.
2. Design a small assessment for each criterion: evidence sought, question or
   work sample, anchored rating and who observes it. Use the same core criteria
   across candidates, with appropriate accommodations and relevant follow-ups.
3. Compare supplied evidence with the anchors. Record the source, observations,
   contrary evidence and uncertainty. Missing evidence is unknown, not failure.
   Do not infer competence from identity, prestige, personality guesses or tone.
4. Keep each evaluator's initial assessment independent before debrief. Resolve
   disagreements through evidence and criteria; do not let seniority or a single
   overall impression silently replace them.
5. For pipeline diagnosis, reconcile the recruiting funnel using dated records
   and explicit stage rules.
   Distinguish a recorded stage from a supported transition, an offer draft from
   an approved/sent offer, and acceptance from confirmed onboarding.
6. Deliver the supported recommendation, decisive evidence, remaining checks and
   next owner. Continue authorized scheduling or document work when its target
   and prerequisites are known; analysis alone grants no outreach or offer power.

Read [methods.md](references/methods.md) for scorecard design, fair evidence
comparison and pipeline calculations. Finish with a usable role plan or decision
packet. Real hiring outcomes require the responsible human decision process;
this method does not certify a candidate or automate selection.
