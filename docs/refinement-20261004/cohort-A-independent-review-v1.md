Broader claims still need broader evidence: an offline coefficient check cannot clear realtime safety, a parser test cannot clear robot hardware, and a rendered locale cannot clear cultural review. The old literal mismatch does not establish a lost permission boundary.

The retained methods are substantive choices: shutdown ordering, independent algorithm oracles, calibrated/grouped predictor evaluation, complete consumer validation, uncertain-effect recovery and release-effect reconciliation are not expendable ceremony. Existing long resources are conditional and discoverable, with important mechanism checks still in entrypoints. No blanket shortening or word target was used. The refinery additions are pack-specific disposition and reviewed-byte/evidence-state rules; other additions change domain-specific lane, timing, support or effect decisions rather than copying a generic agent paragraph across all IDs.
