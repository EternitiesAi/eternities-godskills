# Godskills: product completion, not an acquisition treadmill

## Decision and scope

Dom asked to resume, reassess priorities and complete the best useful product we
can. The starting release is `27152ee22940ee5cb31958eebd34bec48213564d37acaf1c49d2c85a79a62ebc`
at commit `da99c884a278b43ca9c83cde3eff22d5b80b037a`: 93 portable skills in 21
primary categories. Its installed copy validates. The existing broad capabilities,
specialists and optional references are the product; Godagents, a warehouse,
provider account and Keel are not prerequisites.

The useful direction is **a small, dependable capability library that an agent
can discover and apply selectively**. Adding another source or certificate is
not progress unless it fixes a demonstrated gap, improves a useful method or
makes delivery more dependable. Keep good spontaneous model work; guidance has
to earn its context cost.

## Three distinct finish lines

| Track | Finite acceptance condition | Current boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portable product | Coherent documented scope; usable discovery and exact selected-body access; preserved reviewed methods; reproducible package; verified upgrade and rollback evidence | 93-skill release exists; this batch improves selective access and adoption |
| Practical effectiveness | Representative difficult tasks, fixed snapshots, matched baseline/guided runs, independent assessment and quality/resource reporting | Earlier evidence is mixed-positive and bounded; no universal superiority claim |
| Source quarry | Case-specific body review and actual accepted disposition for the fixed corpus | Frozen R10 still has 49,514 open obligations and 163 conflicts; acquisition or navigation work closes none |

These distinctions do not cancel the authorized quarry work or relabel it as
finished. They let a usable release ship without pretending that tens of
thousands of overlapping source obligations are installed skills. Research
continues in a separate queue; it must not obscure the product's current state.

The historical September 6 v1 plan described 44 directories, a Godagents
adapter and a proposed 48-run pilot. Those are not retroactively passed by this
portable 93-skill milestone. This is the current product-facing development
direction under Dom's reassessment request, not a rewrite of that contract or
its evidence. Adapter qualification and the old pilot remain unfulfilled unless
separately demonstrated; the portable product does not require those consumers.

## Ordered priorities

1. **Find and inspect the right method with little overhead.** The starting
   `INDEX.md` is 24,375 bytes and `catalog.json` is 141,957 bytes. Reading either
   whole is unnecessary for a user who first needs a category or one named
   skill. Add compact category browsing and exact full-entrypoint inspection,
   with optional reference pointers rather than automatic reference loading.
   Preserve existing search scores, exclusions, authority boundaries and IDs.
2. **Qualify usefulness, not just prose.** After this access milestone, select
   hard representative tasks where the method could change a real decision or
   artifact. Distinguish discovery failure from guidance failure. Prefer repairs
   supported by those observations over indiscriminate rewriting or new counts.
   Do not tune on a supposedly held-out assessment and keep calling it held-out.
3. **Add depth only where evidence identifies a gap.** Use the existing quarry
   for bounded source reading, independent synthesis and separate review. New
   intake stays inert. No mass installation, source execution or bulk unresolved
   dispositions follows from this plan.

## Current implementation boundary

Additive navigation, a short Markdown starting point, and an exact checked
release are in scope. All 93 existing skill payloads, historical receipts,
protected activation files and unrelated installed skills stay unchanged.
No providers, embeddings, network search, background runners, global instruction
changes or automatic skill activation are needed. Navigation exposes published
methods; it does not decide what the user authorized or prove those methods
outperform an unguided model.

Two separate Sol inspections assess the current discovery and completion gaps.
Their findings inform the candidate and its review. The implementation report
will distinguish fresh checks, preserved limitations and remaining work.
