## What becomes useful

The [accessible user-study method](../product/skills/user-research-and-usability-study/references/accessible-user-study.md)
extends the ordinary interview/usability owner for studies where
participant-selected access arrangements affect what can be observed. It
produces a scoped plan, task/access coverage, data-class agreement, session
observations and denominator-specific synthesis. It keeps offered help, used
help, missing observations and unattempted tasks distinct. A session stop is
separate from what happens to previously collected data. A plan may be useful
without authorizing collection or claiming that inaccessible tasks were tested.

The [staged literature-synthesis method](../product/skills/eternities-logos/references/staged-literature-synthesis.md)
extends Logos for an evidence-grounded, multi-document synthesis. It starts
with the question, corpus and comparison dimensions; builds a claim-linked
outline; expands sections from inspected evidence including counterevidence;
versions anchored section summaries; and reconciles abstract, introduction
and conclusion in both directions. A locator, abstract or secondhand account
is not silently treated as an inspected body. Ordinary copy edits and
single-source summaries do not need this long method.

Neither method requires a provider, personal directory, warehouse, Keel,
Godagents, slash command, extra agent or external service. Both are conditional
references: agents read the existing owner first and load the method only when
the task actually calls for its depth.
