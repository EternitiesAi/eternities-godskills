The source, independent document reviews, full maintained suite and standalone
export are verified. Exact content and qualification coordinates follow;

## What changed

The pack keeps 93 entrypoints across 21 categories. This release deepens existing
owners rather than adding overlapping skill names. The selected method directory
grows from 21 to 29 entries; it is not a count of every procedure in the pack.

| Job | Added conditional method | Existing owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualify a commercial opportunity | Choose discovery questions by the different next actions their answers justify; separate problem, offering fit and adoption feasibility | Agora |
| Get customers to first value | Trace required inputs and actors to a recipient-accepted result, then distinguish blockers and mature versus pending cohorts | Beacon |
| Improve operational throughput | Distinguish ready-work saturation, starvation, downstream blocking and rework before a capacity intervention | Operating cadence |
| Choose the next learning exercise | Resolve competing explanations of a response with a short probe and concrete next-item branches | Evidence-linked learning |
| Build a shader or visual effect | Construct carrier, mask, motion and composition in inspectable stages; isolate draw, shader and compositor faults | Arcadia |
| Tune lighting and materials | Cross reference/target materials with reference/intended illumination before changing a parameter | Arcadia |
| Handle interrupted animation | Separate gameplay authorization, fading skeletal pose, additive layers and movement ownership | Arcadia |
| Recover a frame budget | Separate active work from waits and use a falsifiable resolution, pass or subsystem probe | Arcadia |

These resources load only when the actual task needs them. Existing owner routes,
other methods and ordinary no-skill work remain available.

Discovery also corrects a demonstrated refusal-scope defect. The initial repair
overmatched words such as `fixtures` and `debugger`; independent review caught it.
The narrower repair retains the intended fix/debug forms without those regressions.
Both the failure and the corrected review remain in repository evidence.

## Evidence boundaries

Separate non-author reviews examined the eight methods against existing coverage,
actual selected source bodies and different worked fictional transfer cases. Parent
source checks matched 19 bodies and seven notices. Source identities, original
negative reviews and subsequent correction records remain intact. No upstream code
was executed, no acquired source was installed, and no corpus obligation was closed.

The frozen eight-query development check improved from 7/8 to 8/8 expected-owner
retrieval after integration. It is author-visible routing evidence, not a blinded
model-quality result. The focused discovery and evaluation-setup run passed 73 tests.

A separate four-task matched-agent protocol was prepared, but **no live comparison
was run**: the available launcher injected global instructions and skills, conflicting
with the intended control condition. This limitation was not repaired by changing
global settings or pretending both conditions were raw. Its admission remains HOLD.

Reviewer arithmetic and fictional applications do not prove sales lift, retention,
learning gains, renderer correctness, artistic appeal or better model performance.
Those remain specific opportunities for real consumer evaluation.
