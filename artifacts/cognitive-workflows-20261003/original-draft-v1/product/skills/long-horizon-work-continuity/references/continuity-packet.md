# Continuity packet

Use at a milestone, compaction, interruption, or transfer of ownership when the next actor would otherwise have to reconstruct decisions. Save only to an authorized location; absent one, return the packet to the user. This template provides fields, not storage or a scheduler. Omit inapplicable fields rather than leaving misleading empty assurances.

## Task contract

- Task identity, target identity, packet time and author; objective and observable finish.
- Invariants, exclusions, and governing request or contract locator.
- Current authorization: exact targets and effects, retained decisions, resource ceilings and remaining allowance where known, expiry or invalidation conditions if supplied.
- Current user steering, pause/stop scope, decision owner, and unresolved changes to scope or consequences.
- Host facilities actually available: permitted store, active executor, authoritative observation, authorized runner/wakeup. Name absent facilities and resulting handoff boundary.

## Milestones and ownership

| Milestone | Prerequisites | Deliverable and acceptance evidence | Actual stage | Live owner/observation | Remaining dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | Contract decision | Versioned artifact and distinguishing check | Prepared/implemented/checked/integrated/applied as evidenced | Owner and last confirmation | Open prerequisite or gate |

Include exact affected revision or artifact digest, current branch identity where relevant, pending changes, protected concurrent work, and the next integration destination. A branch name alone is insufficient if its source moved.

## Evidence and effects

- Verified results with command or inspection, revision, outcome, and retrievable receipt.
- Changes awaiting verification and the check each invalidates.
- Rejected or eliminated approach with reason/evidence and the condition that would reopen it.
- Unavailable evidence, conflicting claims, and the least observation needed to resolve them.

| Operation identity | Target/payload identity | Authorized effect and limit | Last observed state | Receipt/source | Reconciliation/next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stable request ID if available | Exact destination and version | Allowed consequence and cost | Intended/requested/acknowledged/running/succeeded/failed/cancelled/unknown | Authoritative locator or unavailable | Read status; hold mutation until resolved |

Never retain credentials or raw sensitive payloads just to make the packet reproducible. Use a permitted reference or digest. If no durable pre-dispatch record exists, say so and retain the uncertainty; do not retroactively claim a reliable operation history.

## Resume decision

- One next ready action, its prerequisites and scope.
- Live checks needed before it: owner, affected revisions, pause/resource state, or an uncertain effect.
- Exact held action and why, plus independent work that can proceed.
- Required finish gates, next owner, and active/paused/blocked/complete state with its observation basis.

On resumption read this packet first, then the specific sources needed to establish those prerequisites. Retrieve older history only to resolve a named gap. If the packet is missing or corrupt, reconstruct from the governing request, current artifacts and authoritative receipts; treat gaps as unknown. Do not invent prior decisions or effects.
