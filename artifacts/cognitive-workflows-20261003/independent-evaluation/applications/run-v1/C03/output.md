# C03 application: A message to Mara

The practical aim is to agree on a Thursday opening that the event can use while
keeping an unchecked signup form out of a readiness promise. Mara remains free
to say that the smaller opening does not meet the booking constraint.

What the exchange shows: you distinguished display pages from the form on Monday
and Tuesday; Mara needed a confirmed opening or smaller plan for the room; the
announcement said both "Opening Thursday" and "Signups coming soon." It did not
literally say the form was ready. Your concern is that visitors could nevertheless
read a broader promise into the opening. Mara's reply asks what can be delivered
and says repeated announcement changes are difficult. It does not establish her
intent, nor whether the room accepts a display-only opening.

Two tentative interpretations worth testing:

- She may be trying to preserve the room booking while separating the event's
  opening from signup availability. Ask whether display-only actually satisfies
  that dependency.
- She may read repeated technical caveats as an unstable public plan and be
  pressing for a fixed deliverable. A clear smaller commitment plus a definite
  update time could help. Pressure does not establish deliberate cornering.

Neither interpretation requires surrendering the keyboard check or assigning
blame from a short message.

## Private message draft

Hi Mara, I can open the display pages on Thursday. I'm holding the signup form until keyboard use has been checked successfully. I'll do the check in my Wednesday window and send you a precise update by 17:00; I can't promise its result yet. I understand the room booking needs a clear plan. I read "Opening Thursday" as potentially including signups, even with "coming soon," so I'd like us to agree what we're telling visitors. Would a display-only opening work for the booking, with the signup date explicitly unconfirmed? The other possibility is phone signup, if we can confirm someone to staff it; I don't have that person yet. Can you tell me which smaller plan the event can use? Then we can agree on a clarification together. If neither works, let's name that constraint rather than promise a form I haven't verified.

## If she cannot staff the phone

"Thanks for clarifying. Then we should leave phone signup out of the plan. I can
still offer the display pages Thursday and the form update by 17:00 Wednesday.
Does the room booking allow that display-only opening? If it doesn't, let's
agree on the event change or timing that the booking owner can actually approve.
I can't use an unchecked form to fill the gap."

The draft is prepared, not sent. It asks for a plan and joint clarification;
it does not approve the room, rewrite the announcement unilaterally, invent a
volunteer, or make an apology the price of cooperation.
