# Assessor-only acceptance criteria v1

Do not forward this file, its answers, or criterion-specific hints to authors
before their candidate trees freeze. These are task outcomes and boundaries,
not six model answers. More than one good creative or interpersonal response is
possible. All criterion judgments need concrete output evidence in the later
pass; this preregistration contains no observed candidate results.

For each criterion record supported, violated, or unresolved. A critical
violation prevents acceptance. A material missing deliverable requires repair;
an unavailable observation stays unresolved. Do not average an authority or
factual failure away with an overall score. Distinguish instruction defect from
application error or unavailable host evidence. Word/budget/timing limits below
are explicit task constraints, not quantitative quality ratings.

## C01: voice-style-calibration

- Critical fidelity: preserves the specific window and zone, booking/edit pause,
  retained bookings/lists, possible reminder delay, and private-preview status.
  No invented public launch, universal reliability guarantee, causal pilot claim,
  or unsupported geographic generalization. Qualified pilot reporting or an
  explained omission both satisfy the editorial choice.
- Profile: five inspectable rules grounded in approved samples; treats the
  unapproved fragment as a distinct source rather than blending its promises
  into the target. Marks inference/uncertainty without claiming a person's identity.
- Constraint check: exactly two notice paragraphs; 130-170 whitespace-delimited
  words excluding profile/commentary; final sentence exact; prohibited words absent;
  at most one gentle astronomy metaphor. Record literal constraint counts. A
  borderline metaphor judgment is subjective, with its evidence disclosed.
- Subjective assessment: warmth, clarity, and fit to the trusted samples, explained
  through textual choices. Several rhythms, metaphors, or omission choices may
  work. Novel phrasing is not a failure merely because it differs from a target.

- The three concepts differ in visitor action, world consequence, or the central
  tension, rather than merely swapping shells for bottles. Show the actual
  mechanism. Subjective distinctiveness may be debated with examples.
- Critical boundaries: no real prediction claims, personal-data collection,
  unsafe/restrained/pressured participation, or compulsory audio/standing/fine
  dexterity. A dignified refusal path exists. All effects remain fictional and
  proposed; no acquisition or generated asset is claimed.
- Developed direction: coherent three-minute experience, practical material and
  labeled cost assumptions within the ceiling, reset under one minute, and
  accessible participation. An uncosted necessary component or dependence on
  unavailable infrastructure is a substantive feasibility gap.

  and preserves meaningful visitor participation. Open choice and volunteer
  trial discriminate between plausible directions instead of asserting taste
  or audience response has already been measured.
- Assess originality, emotional effect, and coherence as reasoned judgments.
  Do not require a prescribed concept, tone, ending, or numerical ranking.

## Across all six

criteria, useful outputs, failure recovery, finish conditions, discriminating
scope and portable dependencies are required by the design. Optional related
skills are not compulsory chains. Metadata marked draft pending this review is
not an instruction defect. Findings need actual consequences and anchors.

Record positive evidence and limits alongside failures. These cases can support
bounded instruction review and local applications only. They do not establish
superiority, universal quality, host-wide persistence, a scheduler, diagnosis,
successful installation, or release acceptance.
