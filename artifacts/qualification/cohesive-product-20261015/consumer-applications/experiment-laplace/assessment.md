# Assessment of the fictional upgrade claim

The supplied records do not support adopting this snapshot for autonomous
production or attributing an improvement to the skill pack. They support keeping
it as a candidate for a fresh, bounded comparison. This assessment uses fictional
fixture data; no evaluation runs, provider calls or production use occurred here.

## What the records support

The author reported 10/12 wins for the pack and 8/12 for raw. Those are reported
totals from the described process, not verified comparable success rates. Case
identities, paired outcomes and valid-run classifications are unavailable.

Recorded input tokens were 20,000 for raw and 14,000 for the pack: 6,000 fewer,
or 30% fewer relative to the recorded raw total. This is an arithmetic statement
about recorded input usage. It does not establish 30% lower charges, equal work,
equal quality, or a reproducible token advantage. Output usage, actual charges,
retry costs, human correction effort and comparable billing conditions are not
provided. Models and provider cache policies differed; cached-input counters
are absent. The magnitude or existence of a cache benefit is unknown.

Local package tests and installation hash checks reportedly passed. That supports
the properties those checks exercised and consistency with the installation's
expected bytes. It does not establish semantic skill quality, publisher
authenticity, real-caller usefulness or safe autonomous operation. The fixture
provides no test logs or manifests for this consumer to verify independently.

## Why the comparison cannot establish an upgrade

| Problem | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Tasks selected after observing failures, hardest failures removed, prompts rewritten until wins appeared | Development and evaluation were mixed; the final sample cannot estimate performance on the intended task population. The discarded pool's complete identity and size are unknown. |
| Raw used M1; pack used M2 | Model and pack effects are confounded. Even a clean comparison would compare two systems, not isolate the skill pack. |
| Author scored with labels visible | Scoring was neither independent nor blinded; no independent adjudication is supplied. |
| Three pack runs exceeded the allowance but counted as wins | The reported outcome fails to distinguish eventual output from completion within the required budget. A new study must predeclare that distinction. |
| Two pack runs reused saved successful outputs | These are not fresh executions in the assigned conditions. Their context and eligibility are not established. |
| Invalid/over-budget cases overlap and IDs were lost | There is no defensible corrected numerator, denominator, paired win table or corrected win rate. Subtracting five from ten would invent disjointness. |
| Cache treatment differed and counters are absent | Cache contribution and comparable billed cost cannot be reconstructed. |
| No real production consumer used this snapshot | Packaging success cannot close the actual adoption or autonomy question. |

The reported 10-versus-8 difference therefore cannot support causality,
statistical superiority or universal capability. Increasing repetitions on the
same selected prompts would not repair selection bias, prompt tuning or model
confounding. Preserve the flawed records as development history; do not relabel
them as an untouched baseline or reconstruct missing IDs from guesses.

## Decision and next evidence

Do not adopt this snapshot for autonomous production on these records. Retain it
as an unqualified candidate. The companion study proposes 12 fresh task pairs
(24 runs), restricted to local artifact work for a proposed internal operations
caller. It fixes model, tools, resources and cache treatment, separates task
selection and scoring from authorship, and retains failures and costs.

The decision it could inform is whether to offer this exact snapshot in a limited,
human-reviewed pilot for the tested workflow. It cannot authorize autonomous
external actions or general adoption across professions. An inconclusive result
can finish with deferral or a narrower revised candidate; it need not trigger an
unbounded search for universal proof.
