# Authored decision case

This is a synthetic arithmetic and routing probe, not a customer outcome or model evaluation.

Brief: one tutorial and one short vertical cut, budget 6,000 units including paid usage. A creator can deliver one contracted role. Availability is confirmed for this synthetic example.

| Creator | Suitable role | Production fee | Paid-usage fee | Audience-fit evidence |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A | Tutorial | 4,000 | 1,000 | Strong, from a supplied dated audience export |
| B | Tutorial | 2,800 | 400 | Adequate, from the same measurement basis |
| C | Vertical cut | 1,800 | 500 | Strong, from the same measurement basis |

The individual preference A does not produce a feasible two-role slate: A+C costs 7,300. B+C costs 5,500 and covers both roles. No unique combined reach can be promised because cross-creator audience overlap was not supplied. If paid usage becomes mandatory but B's rights quote is withdrawn, B+C becomes a provisional option requiring that quote, not an approved 5,500 booking.

Exclusion: a request to rank job applicants is outside this skill. Conflict: a high follower total cannot override a mandatory category-exclusivity conflict. Boundary: a one-creator campaign should not be expanded to three to five creators merely because an upstream example uses that size.

Source contribution: Cloutdesk's two selected bodies provide the brief-to-roster-to-proposal job and audience/fit signals. The budget/coverage combination check, metric-denominator distinction and rights-fee case are independently authored here. The source bodies do not supply an optimizer or prove that this method improves campaign results.
