# Brief to feasible creator slate

Produce the requested shortlist or proposal from a roster or exported table. No agency platform, account or connector is required. A proposed booking is a draft until availability, fees and rights have been confirmed through an authorized channel.

## Match the campaign, then assemble the slate

Extract what must be delivered: audience, channels, content forms, timing, total budget and which usage rights or exclusivity the buyer needs. Separate constraints that disqualify a candidate from preferences that only change the order. If a thin brief leaves a material choice open, show the assumption beside the affected option.

Evaluate supplied audience and work samples against that brief. Keep creator identity separate from audience composition: a creator's age or appearance does not prove their audience demographics. Label the measurement period and denominator. Follower totals, average views, qualified reach and attributable outcomes answer different questions; compare like with like or leave them incomparable. Missing campaign results are unknown, rather than evidence of poor performance.

For each plausible creator, retain the few facts that change selection: relevant audience, a suitable example, deliverables available, quote scope, rights/exclusivity, schedule, and any conflict. Trace a client-facing claim to those facts. Do not invent a weighted score when the brief does not justify its weights.

Select a combination rather than simply taking the highest-ranked individuals. Count shared production charges once and individual rights or paid-usage fees where they apply. Check the aggregate budget and coverage of required content/channel roles. If audience overlap is unmeasured, report summed reach as non-deduplicated; do not promise it as unique people. Leave a gap visible if no feasible slate exists, and offer the smallest brief change that would make one possible.

Write the proposal around why each included creator contributes to this campaign. Include the deliverables, combined price range, rights assumptions, evidence limits and unresolved availability. An alternate should identify which part of the slate it replaces and the effect on coverage or cost. Avoid a fixed number of creators when the job needs a different number.

## Finish

Deliver the requested slate and usable proposal copy, with a compact comparison and a calculation the rep can reproduce. Finish with a feasible draft or a concrete infeasibility explanation. Drafting does not reserve talent, send messages or grant rights.
