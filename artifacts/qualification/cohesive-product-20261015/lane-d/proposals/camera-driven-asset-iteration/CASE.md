# Authored decision case

Synthetic brief: a deliberately abstract courier character looks correct in an isolated turntable, but its outline disappears at the locked orthographic gameplay camera. Its idle asset revision is `mesh-v2`; walking still uses `mesh-v1`.

First reconcile the two runtime references, then compare the same view. If the hat brim is the intended silhouette cue but projects to 0.6 px, increasing its normal-map resolution is not a discriminating repair. Test a shape/value change that survives the target camera. Do not require fingers, photoreal textures or Blender merely because an upstream viewmodel example requires them. A successful static silhouette repair leaves motion deformation and frame cost unmeasured.

Exclusion: a generic request to load an already approved GLB should use the existing real-time asset-integration method. Conflict: the source's blanket rejection of primitive heroes cannot override the author's approved geometric style. Boundary: one authored prop does not require a planner, builder, critic and specialist for every mesh.

Integration disposition: propose a short conditional authoring extension to Eternities Arcadia, adjacent to its existing `realtime-asset-integration.md`; avoid adding another general asset-import skill. Source references and final hashes are in the lane-wide decision and read-set ledgers.
