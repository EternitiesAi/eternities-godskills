# Authored worked revision

This miniature is original to the proposal. Intended effect: a quiet recognition that the narrator's brother may have returned. The author knows the brother's old custom; the first version does not tell the reader enough to share the recognition.

Before:

> Mara found the cup upside down beside the window. She locked the door and began to cry. Her brother had returned.

Possible revision:

> As children they had left their cups upside down when they meant to come back. Mara had stopped doing it the winter her brother vanished. Now a cup stood upside down beside the window. She touched the dry ring beneath it, then unfastened the door.

The revision gives the reader the private custom before the new cup, allowing an inference without certifying who placed it. The changed door action also carries Mara's response. Whether the more explicit setup suits the surrounding voice remains an artistic decision; a different brief might prefer the first version's abruptness.

Conflict: if the canon says this brother never used that custom, the repair must use an existing shared signal or propose a canon change explicitly. Exclusion: a request to shorten a factual incident report needs a different method. Boundary: a requested narrator who states emotions directly should retain that stylistic option. An editor with access to the twist can explain the inference path but cannot certify a blind first-reader response.
