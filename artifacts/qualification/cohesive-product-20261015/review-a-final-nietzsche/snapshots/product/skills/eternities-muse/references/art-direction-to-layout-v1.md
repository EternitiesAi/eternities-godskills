# Turn art direction into a working layout

Use when a visual direction must become an interface or repeatable composition,
especially when attractive mood references have not resolved actual layout.
Reuse an approved direction for a narrow implementation or repair.

## Give the direction a job

Name the audience's first useful action and the feeling the brief asks the
experience to carry. Gather representative real content: an ordinary item, a
long title, a missing image, a dense result and an important empty/error state.
Do not let flattering placeholder content decide the design.

When direction is open, develop a few genuinely different composition ideas
against the same content and viewport. Vary hierarchy, spatial rhythm, image
role, typography or interaction emphasis; swapping palettes on the same cards
does not explore a different direction. A verbal description can select a
candidate, but render or sketch a representative moment before treating the
layout as resolved. Keep intentional atmosphere, strangeness or playfulness
when it serves the brief.

## Derive rules from the chosen composition

Write a visual law that makes a difficult design choice easier. “Premium and
clean” cannot decide anything; “a quiet reading field with one oversized active
work and compact supporting metadata” can decide scale, density and emphasis.
State where the law yields to task clarity, access or dense content.

Turn that law into a small working grammar: type roles, spacing relationships,
surface and contrast roles, image treatment, component hierarchy and one or two
distinctive moments where useful. Prefer roles over a pile of unrelated values.
Assign deliberate exceptions rather than forcing every screen into a hero.
Do not copy a reference's brand marks or distinctive assets without appropriate
rights; use the compositional relationship you can independently realize.

Choose layout changes from content pressure. Determine what wraps, stacks,
condenses or moves when width shrinks; retain reading order and the primary action.
A narrow screen need not be a scaled-down desktop. Show how long text, missing
media and loading affect the same grammar. Keep status legible without relying
only on color or an ornamental animation.

## Inspect the artifact, then revise its cause

Build or render the requested surface with the real content and relevant states.
Inspect the first visual read, the next action, comfortable text measure, grouping,
focus and the intended expressive quality. A source token list cannot establish
the result. If everything competes for attention, revise hierarchy before adding
more decoration. If the chosen rule breaks a dense task, alter its application
instead of shrinking all text or suppressing necessary information.

Illustration: for an archive of night-sky observations, “one observation as the
focal object; instruments and dates as marginal notes” can support a spacious
detail view and a compact, aligned index. Long observation titles wrap in the
index while dates retain their own column until the content needs stacking.
The law connects the views without making the index a gallery of giant cards.
This is an authored direction, not evidence of audience preference.

Return the actual requested design or implementation with its governing rule,
reusable decisions, observed states and remaining acceptance questions. Do not
substitute a mood board for a requested working interface.
