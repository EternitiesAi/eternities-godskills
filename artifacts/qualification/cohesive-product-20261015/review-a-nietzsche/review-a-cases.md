# Independent reviewer applications

These cases were authored and worked by the reviewer after reading the seven method bodies and owner entrypoints. Lane A's `self-applications.md`, its author answers and `review-cases.json` were not opened. The method examples themselves were read as instructions, not treated as observed outcomes. All case people, organizations, products, records and traces below are synthetic. This is an independent instruction/application review, not a blind model comparison or evidence of customer, player, listener or learner outcomes.

`probe-results.json` contains nine executed arithmetic assertions, seven executed wiring checks and 21 metadata consumer probes, plus a separate hypothetical trigger-repair experiment. Textual applications below are reviewer-worked; they are not runtime launches or observations of real people.

## Commercial driver diagnosis

**Direct application.** Opening monthly run rate is 200: account A contributes 120 and B contributes 80. During the window A expands by 30, B contracts by 10 and then churns its remaining 70; new account C contributes 40 and dormant account D reactivates with 20. Closing run rate is 210. The full bridge is `200 + 40 + 30 + 20 - 10 - 70 = 210`. The opening cohort closes at 150, giving 75% net retention for that cohort. Positive new/reactivation contributions of 60 sit outside it.

**Paraphrase.** “Our subscription total rose, but old accounts are shrinking; what should we investigate?” leads to the same split: entrants mask opening-cohort losses. The next discriminating check is the source/eligibility and account history behind B's loss; this arithmetic does not establish dissatisfaction or justify more acquisition spending.

**Conflict.** The method's phrase “mutually exclusive events over the same opening customer population” conflicts with adding C and D. Applying that population restriction literally produces 150 and a residual of 60 against the reported close. Both the correct bridge and the counterexample arithmetic executed successfully. This is a method-text repair, not a failing arithmetic implementation.

**Boundary.** Two customer segments maintain conversion at 80% and 20%. A mix change from 100/100 entrants to 20/180 changes aggregate conversion from 50% to 26% without any within-segment decline. A proposed onboarding rewrite needs evidence beyond that aggregate change. The ordered quantity/price decomposition also closes on a separate 10→12 quantity and 4→5 price fixture; its accounting interaction allocation is not a causal estimate.

**Exclusion.** “What is 12 × 5?” needs the answer 60, without a driver bridge or company action packet. The new method states that exclusion.

**Disposition:** REPAIR the reporting-population sentence. Remaining mechanism is useful and bounded.

## Positioning to message

**Direct application.** Supplied product facts: a field-inspection tool records readings and notes offline and exports CSV; it has no automatic cross-device sync, and no measured time/error benefit. The current alternative is paper notes later entered into a spreadsheet. Buyer concern: recording observations where connectivity is unreliable. Requested output is a landing section.

Reviewer output:

> Log field checks without a connection.
>
> Keep readings and notes in one offline inspection record, then export a CSV for your team's existing process. Automatic cross-device sync is not included.
>
> See an example record.

The synthetic fixture supplies a sample record as the proof asset; the CTA is not a claim that an unprovided production demo exists. A supporting note identifies the existing export capability and leaves outcome savings unmeasured.

**Paraphrase.** “Our offer sounds interchangeable; say what changes for this buyer” selects the buyer/workaround/consequence distinction, not a new slogan taxonomy.

**Conflict.** The requested phrase “Cut errors in half” has no supplied evidence. Replace it with the narrower inspectable offline/export promise; keep the unsupported figure out of customer copy. If automatic sync is decisive, name that mismatch rather than expanding the claim.

**Boundary.** With no customer interviews, buyer pain remains a provisional hypothesis. Two copy variants are warranted only if a real framing choice remains. Reader comprehension would not establish willingness to pay.

**Exclusion.** Correcting a spelling error in an approved headline does not need positioning. The body says this; the lexical shortlist can still return Beacon, so the host's need/fit decision remains essential.

**Disposition:** method body and owner fit READY; v2 metadata needs the targeted discovery repair documented separately.

## Art direction to layout

**Direct application.** Brief: an observation archive should feel spacious and slightly strange, but its 320-pixel index must show long titles, dates, missing images and a retryable error. Approved law: a quiet reading field with one focal observation; supporting records stay compact.

Reviewer composition sketch: with 16-pixel side padding, 288 pixels remain. A title column can flex beside an 88-pixel date column with a 12-pixel gap, leaving 188 pixels for the title. Long text wraps; at a still narrower usable width the date stacks after the title in source order. Missing media becomes a labeled absence rather than a blank click target. The error displays text and a retry control, not color alone. The detail view can retain one large focal image while the index uses compact rows. This is a sketch and arithmetic, not a rendered interface or access acceptance result.

**Paraphrase.** “Turn these mood references into a real layout” requires representative content and a composition, rather than a palette-only comparison.

**Conflict.** A visually impressive oversized card that hides the primary archive action fails the task. The law explicitly yields to clarity and density; its exception is useful rather than requiring a second universal system.

**Boundary.** An approved direction does not need three new competing moods. A long unbroken identifier needs a wrapping decision; shrinking all text to preserve a hero would address the wrong layer. Final responsiveness and keyboard order remain untested without a build/render.

**Exclusion.** A one-margin edit in an approved component can proceed without art-direction exploration.

**Disposition:** body and owner fit READY; v2 metadata needs the targeted discovery repair.

## Core-loop playtest

**Direct application.** Synthetic traces describe a quiet lens game. The input responds and the alignment cue is clear. Left and right choices both reveal the same result, despite a stated goal of discovering distinct relations. The trace includes a facilitator saying “keep playing” before cycles three and four.

Reviewer decision: the unexplained sameness is an agency/development question, not an input-latency defect. Try one consequence that changes the next relation, or explicitly settle on an expressive/contemplative toy if that is the intended promise. Do not infer voluntary continuation from prompted cycles three and four.

**Paraphrase.** “The prototype works but feels flat after one repetition” asks what repetition adds, before multiplying content.

**Conflict.** The creative brief rejects combat and scoring. The method permits sustained mood and expressive choices, so adding points is not required. A cue change would be warranted only if the player could not perceive alignment.

**Boundary.** Without a player, this is a designer walkthrough of synthetic traces. A machine state-transition test cannot turn it into observed enjoyment. New build observations must keep their revision and assistance conditions distinct.

**Exclusion.** A settled null-check repair in an asset loader does not need a new loop study.

**Disposition:** body, original owner fit and sampled v2 discovery READY at the documented evidence level.

## Supplied-audio editorial mix

**Direct application.** Synthetic edit brief: an authorized interview has a bell cue masking a name; a pause sounds rushed. The transcript says “We have not confirmed the return date.” Candidate edit removes “not” and shortens the pause. A waveform-only check reports a lower sample peak.

Reviewer decision: preserve the negation, restore enough timing for the intended statement, and test a local bell move/attenuation before lowering the whole narration. Keep source in/out mappings. A lower level cannot by itself reconstruct already clipped speech, and a sample-peak result is not a true-peak measurement. The requested final check belongs on the encoded output, with actual listening conditions recorded.

**Paraphrase.** “Improve the listening result around that name” retains editorial meaning and audibility as separate causes, not a universal denoising recipe.

**Conflict.** With playback unavailable, provide an edit proposal and pending listening checks; do not claim audible improvement. If the name remains unintelligible with music removed, leave its uncertainty rather than fabricate speech or a transcript.

**Boundary.** No fixed platform loudness target is supplied, so none is invented. No real audio was decoded, edited or heard in this review; the case challenges the stated decision boundary only.

**Exclusion.** New song generation or voice impersonation is outside this supplied-audio method.

**Disposition:** body and owner fit READY; v2 metadata needs the targeted discovery repair.

## Practice and transfer

**Direct application.** Goal: compare quantities using the relevant denominator. Worked density example: object A has 100 g/50 cm³ = 2 g/cm³; object B has 300 g/300 cm³ = 1 g/cm³. The heavier object is less dense. Ask the learner to choose before exposing the result.

Contrast/fresh item: C is 240 g/120 cm³ and D is 180 g/60 cm³. Expected densities are 2 and 3, so the lighter D is denser. If the explanation says “C is denser because it weighs more,” revisit the per-volume comparison. If the setup is correct but division is wrong, give focused arithmetic support. Do not infer a misconception from the chosen letter alone.

**Paraphrase.** “Give me items that show whether they can choose the method rather than copy the example” calls for a consequential feature change and a fresh attempt after feedback.

**Conflict.** A correct response after the teacher reveals it is not an independent solution. Keep an authorized reading aid available; it is not a cue to withdraw after one correct answer.

**Boundary.** A changed unit-cost context can ask for price per 100 g: 500 g at 6 units costs 1.2 per 100 g; 750 g at 8 costs about 1.067. The quantity and denominators are explicit. Correcting these keys establishes artifact arithmetic, not transfer or durable learning in a person. No later retention check took place.

**Exclusion.** A substitute-facilitator handoff uses the owner's existing reporting route, without rebuilding practice. The owner can remain a search candidate while this new reference stays unloaded.

**Disposition:** body and owner fit READY; v2 metadata needs the targeted discovery repair.

## Contrastive voice calibration

**Direct application.** Authorized synthetic team samples use short status-first sentences followed by a limit or next step: “Payment is pending. We'll update you when the bank confirms.” “We've opened a repair request. The start date is tentative.” “Uploads resumed at 09:40; earlier items remain queued.” The evidence supports direct status/constraint presentation, not false certainty or a universal ban on imagery.

Source message: “We may reopen submissions on Monday after the maintainer checks the fix. No date is confirmed.” Reviewer adaptation: “Submissions may reopen on Monday after the maintainer checks the fix. The date isn't confirmed.” This preserves the modal, date, prerequisite, uncertainty and action state.

**Paraphrase.** “These drafts sound like exaggerated mannerisms; derive usable edits from our examples” calls for bounded operations and a fresh-register check.

**Conflict.** “Submissions reopen Monday” loses uncertainty; “we checked the fix” invents a completed action. Neither is a style improvement the method permits.

**Boundary.** A metaphor in one launch sample is not a recurring incident-report trait. A user correction limited to the current message need not become a global rule. This review did not obtain the intended author's acceptance.

**Exclusion.** A one-line friendly rewrite without a profile can use ordinary writing competence. Voice synthesis is outside this written-voice method.

**Disposition:** body, owner fit and sampled v2 discovery READY at the documented evidence level.
