## Negative and unresolved evidence

The baseline already supported implementation, continuity, explicit authority,
uncertain-effect reconciliation and minimal lookup. The candidate does not claim
to invent those capabilities. Its substantive additions are caller completion,
discriminating probes, task-level paired analysis, explicit recovery lookup
failure classes, selective dependency revalidation and mixed-batch outcomes.

These cases do not establish that a fresh agent selects or follows the methods,
that the candidate outperforms the baseline, or that all scientific and framework
claims in retained skills are correct. Independent behavioral review remains
open. The 47 retain decisions mean no concrete entrypoint defect justified a
change in this bounded audit, not renewed certification.
