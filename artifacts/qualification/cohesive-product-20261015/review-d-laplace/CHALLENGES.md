## 2. Camera-critical asset: choose the repair layer

Fixture: a deliberately geometric courier must be recognizable by a triangular cloak corner at a fixed orthographic play camera. Idle resolves to revision r8; walking resolves to r7. At the intended resolution the corner projects to 0.7 px. A proposed repair is a larger normal map. A material shimmer also depends on an authoring-tool frame handler.

Actual reviewer output, an iteration proposal:

1. Reconcile the walking and idle runtime references to the intended r8 before judging a geometry change. Keep the camera, resolution, framing and lighting fixed.
2. Compare an unlit outline. If the corner is absent in that outline, test a localized shape/proportion change that makes the intended cue persist at the play camera; a normal-map resolution change does not alter the outline. If the outline is already distinct and only its surface merges into the ground, test value/material separation instead.
3. Keep the approved geometric style. Do not add realistic anatomy, select Blender, rebuild the entire mesh or require a universal triangle count.
4. Recapture idle and walking in that same game view. A static outline pass does not clear the shoulder deformation seen only during walking.
5. Treat the shimmer as a separate target-behavior requirement: identify a supported baked/runtime representation, then observe it in the target. An exported file does not demonstrate a frame handler survived.

Criteria: intended revision in both states; cue visibly separable in the agreed view; no new motion gap; target shimmer behavior demonstrated if required. No arbitrary “two pixels always passes” rule. Runtime cost requires the target build/device/workload and a measurement; triangle reduction alone cannot pass it.

Result: concrete diagnostic/iteration decisions are produced. No mesh, renderer, image, animation or frame-time result was available or created. All visual acceptance remains pending.

## 3. Causal design: resist plausible wrong shortcuts

Case A: 100 eligible clinics are randomly assigned an invitation, but only some accept. Synthetic supplied summary means are 42 for assigned invitation and 38 for assigned control, in the stated outcome unit and horizon. Actual arithmetic difference: 4 units for assignment groups. The requested assignment-effect target is not an uptake effect. Before inference, verify assignment records, attrition and interference, and use clinic-level uncertainty appropriate to the assignment. No variance, patient rows or valid confidence interval is supplied; no estimator was executed. Restricting to accepters would change both comparison and selection assumptions.

Case B: a policy starts in 2020 and records exist for 2017, 2020 and 2024. Actual event times in years are -3, 0, 4, not row offsets. A pre-trend p-value of 0.7 does not establish parallel trends. Already-treated regions are not automatically valid controls for later cohorts; define cohort/time comparisons and anticipation assumptions. If the fixture supplies no credible untreated comparison, report the change descriptively rather than manufacture a DiD estimate.

Case C: an instrument has a large first stage but also directly changes access to the outcome service. Reject an exclusion-based IV claim despite the first stage. Similarly, matching/weighting agreement cannot recover unsupported treated units; trimming requires a supported-subset target, not the original population label.

Result: design choice and supported claim change. Calendar arithmetic and one synthetic difference are actual computations; assignment validity, estimator correctness and real causal effects are not validated. These cases test the method's reasoning boundaries, not coverage of every causal design.

## 5. Reveal revision: repair inference on the page

Invented canon: Iona and her missing sister used three uneven taps as a private signal. Anyone could now imitate it; the sister's return must remain uncertain. Iona knows the custom, but the reader has not encountered it.

Original:

> Three uneven taps came from the stairwell. Iona took her coat. Her sister was home.

Actual revised passage:

> When they were children, her sister had knocked three uneven taps to tell Iona the coast was clear. Tonight the sound came from the stairwell. Iona took her coat, but left the chain on the door.

Information trace: the opening supplies the private signal; the next sentence supplies its recurrence; the coat expresses readiness while the chain preserves doubt. The narration no longer certifies the sister's return. It does not add an exclusive or magical rule that nobody else can reproduce the signal. Iona's belief and the world's truth remain separate.

Downstream check: a later line “she knew with certainty who waited outside” now needs an independent basis or revision; a branch that skipped the childhood explanation cannot be assumed to share the inference. If direct certainty were the author's intended voice/effect, this revision would not be an improvement for that brief.

Result: a real text revision and editorial account were delivered. The hypothesis is that the cue is now inferable without revealing the visitor. No human reader response, suspense rating or stylistic superiority was observed.
